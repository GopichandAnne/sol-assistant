# Flexible and Remote Working Policy

**Northwind Group** · Policy HR-11 · Version 2.4
Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: People Operations · Review date: 1 March 2027

---

## 1. Our working pattern

Northwind operates a hybrid pattern. Colleagues in office-based roles attend their contractual office **three days per week**, of which **Tuesday and Thursday are fixed** company-wide anchor days. The third day is agreed within the team.

Teams may agree a different anchor pattern with department head approval where client or shift commitments require it. The agreement is recorded in Workday and reviewed every six months.

## 2. Fully remote roles

A small number of roles are designated fully remote at the point of hire. These are listed in the job advert and the contract. An existing office-based role is not converted to fully remote except through the formal flexible working request process in section 5.

Fully remote colleagues are expected on site for:

- Team onboarding weeks for new joiners in their team
- The twice-yearly department gathering
- Any meeting where a department head has stated attendance in person is required, with at least 15 working days' notice

Travel to these is booked under FIN-02 and is not a commute.

## 3. Remote working allowance

Colleagues working two or more days a week from home receive a **£32 per month** allowance toward heating, power and broadband, paid through payroll. It is taxable and appears on your payslip as "Home working".

A one-off **£350 equipment allowance** is available to all colleagues after probation, for a chair, desk, monitor or similar. Claim it through the expenses system under category HOMEOFFICE, attaching a receipt. Items purchased remain Northwind property and must be returned or bought at written-down value on leaving.

## 4. Working from abroad

Colleagues may work from another country for up to **20 working days** in any rolling 12-month period, subject to:

- Line manager approval, and notification to People Operations at least **four weeks** in advance
- The country being on the approved list maintained by People Operations
- Working hours overlapping the team's core hours by at least four hours a day
- The colleague remaining tax resident in their country of employment

Periods over 20 days create tax and social-security obligations for Northwind and are not approved. This limit is cumulative across the 12 months, not per trip.

Working from a country not on the approved list is not permitted under any circumstances, including for personal or family reasons.

## 5. Formal flexible working requests

Any colleague may make a statutory flexible working request from their first day of employment. Requests may cover hours, days, times or location.

The process:

1. Submit in writing through Workday, stating the change requested and the date you would like it to start
2. People Operations acknowledges within **5 working days**
3. A meeting is held with your line manager within **20 working days**
4. A decision is given in writing within **10 working days** of that meeting
5. If refused, you may appeal in writing within **10 working days** to the department head

The whole process, including any appeal, completes within **two months** of the original request unless you agree an extension.

You may make **two statutory requests** in any 12-month period.

## 6. Core hours

Core hours are **10:00 to 15:00** in your contractual work location's time zone. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours wherever possible. Outside core hours, colleagues manage their own time subject to delivering their work and being contactable as agreed with their team.

There is no expectation to respond to messages outside your working hours. Colleagues in different time zones should use scheduled send.

## 7. Equipment and security

Work is carried out on Northwind-issued equipment only. Company data must not be stored on personal devices, and personal cloud accounts must not be used for company documents. See IT-03 Acceptable Use.

Where you work from home, you are responsible for a workspace that is safe. A display screen equipment self-assessment is completed annually through Workday.
