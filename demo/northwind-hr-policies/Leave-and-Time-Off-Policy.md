# Leave and Time Off Policy

**Northwind Group** · Policy HR-04 · Version 3.2
Effective 1 April 2026 · Owner: People Operations · Review date: 1 April 2027

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how paid and unpaid leave is accrued, requested and approved at Northwind Group. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term colleagues in the UK and Ireland. Contractors engaged through an agency are covered by their agency's terms, not this policy.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time colleagues receive **26 days** of paid annual leave per holiday year, in addition to public holidays. The holiday year runs from **1 April to 31 March**.

Part-time colleagues receive a pro-rata entitlement, rounded up to the nearest half day.

Entitlement increases with continuous service:

| Completed service | Annual entitlement |
| --- | --- |
| Under 3 years | 26 days |
| 3 to 6 years | 28 days |
| Over 6 years | 30 days |

The increase takes effect on the **1 April following** the service anniversary, not on the anniversary itself.

## 3. Carry-over

Up to **6 days** of unused annual leave may be carried into the next holiday year. Carried-over days must be taken by **30 June**, after which they lapse and are not paid in lieu.

Carry-over above 6 days requires written approval from a Director and is granted only where leave could not be taken for business reasons. Approval must be recorded in Workday before 31 March.

Colleagues on long-term sick leave or family leave during the holiday year may carry over their full untaken entitlement, which must be used within 18 months of the end of that holiday year.

## 4. Requesting leave

Leave is requested in Workday and requires approval from your direct line manager.

Notice required is **twice the length of the leave requested**, to a minimum of one week. A five-day absence therefore needs two weeks' notice. Requests submitted with less notice may still be approved at the manager's discretion.

Requests of **10 consecutive working days or more** additionally require approval from your department head.

## 5. Blackout periods

Annual leave is not normally approved during:

- The first two weeks of February (year-end close, Finance only)
- The last full week of September (peak trading, Operations and Customer Service only)

These restrictions do not apply to leave booked and approved before the blackout period was announced.

## 6. Public holidays

Colleagues receive the public holidays of their contractual work location. Where a colleague works a pattern that does not include the day a public holiday falls on, a day in lieu is added to their entitlement.

Northwind additionally closes between Christmas and New Year. These days are **not deducted** from annual leave entitlement.

## 7. Sickness during annual leave

Where a colleague falls ill during booked annual leave and provides a fit note covering the period, those days may be reclassified as sick leave and returned to the annual leave balance. The request must be made within **7 days** of returning to work.

## 8. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to four weeks may be requested after two years' continuous service. It requires department head approval and does not interrupt continuity of service. Pension contributions are paused for the period; private medical cover continues.
