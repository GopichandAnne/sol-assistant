# New Joiner Onboarding Checklist

**Northwind Group** · Guide HR-20 · Version 1.8
Effective 1 May 2026 · Owner: People Operations

---

## Before day one

The hiring manager completes these. People Operations chases anything outstanding at five working days before the start date.

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed contract returned | People Operations | 10 working days before start |
| Right to work check completed | People Operations | Before start date, no exceptions |
| Equipment ordered through the Service Desk | Hiring manager | 10 working days before start |
| Accounts requested (Workday, Microsoft 365, team systems) | Hiring manager | 5 working days before start |
| Buddy assigned | Hiring manager | 5 working days before start |
| First-week calendar built | Hiring manager | 3 working days before start |

Equipment ordered with fewer than 10 working days' notice may not arrive for the start date. The Service Desk holds a small buffer stock of standard laptops for genuine emergencies, released by Service Desk manager approval only.

## Day one

- Arrive at 09:30, not 09:00 — the first half hour is for the team, not for you
- ID badge and building access from Reception, bring photo ID
- Laptop handover and sign-in with a temporary password, set MFA immediately
- Health and safety briefing, and display screen equipment self-assessment
- Lunch with your buddy, expensed by the hiring manager under category ONBOARD

## First week

- Meet your team one to one, 30 minutes each
- Read HR-04 Leave, HR-11 Flexible and Remote Working, IT-03 Acceptable Use
- Complete mandatory training in Workday: Information Security, Data Protection, Anti-Bribery, Health and Safety
- Agree your probation objectives with your line manager in writing

Mandatory training must be finished within **10 working days** of starting. Incomplete training is escalated to the department head at 15 days and suspends system access at 20 days.

## Probation

Standard probation is **6 months** for all colleagues, and **3 months** for internal moves.

Reviews happen at the end of month 1, month 3 and month 5. The month 5 review is the decision point, so any concern should be raised long before it.

Probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months, where a specific concern is documented and a colleague has been given a fair chance to address it. The extension is confirmed in writing before the original probation end date.

Notice during probation is **one week** from either side. After probation, notice is as stated in your contract, normally one month, rising to three months for roles at Principal level and above.

## Pay and benefits

Payday is the **26th of each month**, or the preceding working day where the 26th falls on a weekend or public holiday.

Benefits start on your first day, except private medical cover, which starts on the **first of the month following** your start date. If you start on the 3rd of March, cover begins on 1 April.

Pension auto-enrolment happens after **3 months**. Northwind matches contributions to **6%**, and you may opt into a higher personal contribution at any time through Workday. Colleagues may opt out of auto-enrolment but re-enrolment happens automatically every three years by law.

## The 30-day check-in

People Operations holds a 30-minute conversation with every new joiner at 30 days. It is not a performance review, it does not go to your manager, and its purpose is to find out what we got wrong about your first month so the next person has it better.
