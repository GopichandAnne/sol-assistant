# Expenses and Travel Policy

**Northwind Group** · Policy FIN-02 · Version 5.0
Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Finance · Review date: 1 January 2027

---

## 1. Principle

Northwind reimburses reasonable costs necessarily incurred in doing your job. Where this policy is silent, apply the test a Director would apply: could this be explained to a colleague without embarrassment?

## 2. Approval thresholds

| Amount (per claim) | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to £75 | No pre-approval; line manager approves the claim |
| £75 to £500 | Line manager pre-approval, in writing |
| £500 to £2,500 | Department head pre-approval |
| Over £2,500 | Finance Director pre-approval |

A cost may not be split across claims to stay under a threshold. Where a single trip totals more than £2,500, the whole trip needs Finance Director approval even if each element is smaller.

## 3. Submission deadline

Claims must be submitted within **60 days** of the date the cost was incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days are paid only with Finance Director approval, and claims over **90 days** old are not paid at all.

Expenses are reimbursed with the payroll run following approval. The payroll cut-off is the **18th of each month**.

## 4. Meals and subsistence

Per-diem rates apply when travelling overnight on Northwind business. Receipts are not required within the per diem; costs above it are not reimbursed.

| Location | Per diem (24 hours) |
| --- | --- |
| UK outside London | £45 |
| London | £62 |
| Ireland and EU | €70 |
| United States | $85 |
| Rest of world | £70 equivalent |

Where a meal is provided (by a hotel, a conference or a client), reduce the per diem by **£12 for breakfast, £18 for lunch and £25 for dinner**.

Alcohol is not reimbursable except as part of a client entertainment claim approved in advance under section 7.

## 5. Travel

Rail travel should be booked standard class. First class is permitted only where the total fare is lower than the standard fare available at the time of booking, and the comparison must be attached to the claim.

Air travel is economy class for flights under **6 hours** of scheduled flying time, and premium economy for longer flights. Business class requires Finance Director approval, granted only for flights over 9 hours where the colleague is expected to work on the day of arrival.

Mileage in a private vehicle is reimbursed at **45p per mile** for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and **25p per mile** thereafter. Passenger supplement is 5p per mile per colleague carried.

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable where public transport is unavailable, where the journey is before 06:00 or after 22:00, or where you are carrying equipment.

## 6. Accommodation

Book through the Northwind travel portal where possible. Room-rate caps, excluding breakfast and local taxes:

- London: £220 per night
- UK other: £150 per night
- New York and San Francisco: $350 per night
- Other international: £180 per night

Where no room is available within the cap, book the nearest available and note the reason on the claim.

## 7. Client entertainment

Client entertainment requires **pre-approval from a department head**, whatever the amount, and the claim must record every attendee by name and organisation. Northwind colleagues must not outnumber client attendees.

Gifts to clients over **£50** in value require Finance Director approval and are recorded in the Gifts and Hospitality register. Gifts of cash or cash equivalents are never permitted.

## 8. Not reimbursable

- Parking and speeding fines, and congestion charge penalties
- Personal travel insurance (corporate cover applies to all business travel)
- In-room entertainment, minibar, spa and gym charges
- Flight upgrades purchased personally at the airport
- Home broadband (covered by the remote working allowance in HR-11)

## 9. Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued at department head request to colleagues who travel more than four times a year. Card statements must be reconciled within **30 days**. Two consecutive unreconciled statements result in the card being suspended.
