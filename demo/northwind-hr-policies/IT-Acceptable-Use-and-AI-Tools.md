# IT Acceptable Use and AI Tools

**Northwind Group** · Policy IT-03 · Version 4.1
Effective 1 February 2026 · Owner: Information Security · Review date: 1 August 2026

---

## 1. Scope

This policy covers all use of Northwind systems, devices, networks and data, by colleagues, contractors and anyone else granted access. It sits alongside the Data Protection Policy (LEG-01).

## 2. Accounts and access

Accounts are personal and must not be shared, including with your own manager. Where someone needs access to your work, request delegated access rather than sharing credentials.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all accounts. The approved second factor is the Microsoft Authenticator app. **SMS is not an approved second factor** at Northwind and will be removed from any account where it is found.

Access is reviewed quarterly by system owners. Access not used for **90 days** is revoked automatically, and reinstating it requires a fresh request.

## 3. Passwords

Passwords are managed in the company password manager. Minimum length is **16 characters** for standard accounts and **20 characters** for administrative accounts. Rotation is not required on a schedule; passwords are changed on evidence of compromise.

Reusing a Northwind password on any personal service is a disciplinary matter.

## 4. Devices

Only Northwind-issued devices may access company data. Personal devices may be used for:

- Microsoft Authenticator
- Calendar and email through the Outlook mobile app with Intune protection enabled

Nothing else. In particular, company documents must not be opened on a personal laptop, and company data must not be copied to personal storage or personal cloud accounts.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the Service Desk **within 4 hours**, at any hour, including weekends. The out-of-hours number is on the back of your ID badge.

## 5. Generative AI tools

Northwind supports the use of approved AI tools. The current approved list is:

- Microsoft 365 Copilot, on your Northwind account
- GitHub Copilot, for colleagues in Engineering with a licence
- The Northwind Assistant, on approved internal channels

Using any other generative AI service with Northwind information is not permitted. This includes free consumer tiers of otherwise reputable products, because their terms typically permit training on submitted content.

### What must never go into any AI tool

- Personal data about colleagues, candidates or customers
- Anything covered by a client NDA, or bearing a client's name alongside commercial terms
- Credentials, keys, tokens or connection strings
- Unreleased financial results, and anything marked Commercial in Confidence
- Source code from repositories marked restricted

### What you remain responsible for

Output from an AI tool is your work. You are accountable for its accuracy, and for its licensing where it produces code. AI-generated material must not be presented to a client as reviewed work unless a named colleague has actually reviewed it.

Where AI has materially produced client deliverable content, note that in the engagement record. Several client contracts require disclosure.

## 6. Email and messaging

Be careful with external forwarding. Auto-forwarding rules to external addresses are blocked at the tenant level; requests for an exception go to Information Security and are rarely granted.

Report phishing with the Report Message button in Outlook rather than deleting it. Reporting is what lets the rest of the company be protected within minutes.

## 7. Software

Install software from the Company Portal. Anything not in the portal needs a request to the Service Desk, which checks licensing and security. Do not use personal licences for work, and do not use work licences for personal projects.

Browser extensions are a common route for data loss. Only extensions on the approved list may be installed, and the list is short by design.

## 8. Monitoring

Northwind monitors its systems for security and legal compliance. Monitoring is proportionate and is not used to measure individual productivity. Where monitoring identifies a potential policy breach, it is handled under the Disciplinary Procedure, with the colleague told what was found.

## 9. Reporting a security concern

Report anything that looks wrong to the Service Desk, or to security@northwind.example for anything sensitive. Reporting a genuine concern in good faith never results in disciplinary action, even where it turns out you caused the problem. We would far rather know.

---

*Breaches of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including dismissal. Questions to Information Security.*
