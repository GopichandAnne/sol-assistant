# Statement of Work — ENG-2411

**Client:** Meridian Media Group
**Engagement:** FAST channel migration and ad-tech consolidation
**Prepared:** 18 February 2026 · **Signed:** 11 March 2026
**Sol partner:** Marcus Bell · **Delivery lead:** Priya Shah

---

## 1. Background

Meridian operates fourteen free ad-supported streaming television (FAST) channels across three distribution platforms, each with its own scheduling tool, ad-insertion path and reporting. Channel launches take eleven weeks and depend on four people who each hold part of the process in their heads.

Meridian asked Sol to consolidate onto a single scheduling and ad-decision stack, and to reduce channel launch time to under three weeks.

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

- Current-state assessment of all fourteen channels, including ad-break conventions and SCTE-35 marker practice
- Target architecture for scheduling, playout hand-off and server-side ad insertion
- Migration of all fourteen channels to the consolidated stack
- Reporting rebuild: one revenue and fill-rate view across platforms
- Runbook and handover to Meridian's channel operations team

**Explicitly out of scope**

- Content rights clearance and metadata remediation
- Any change to Meridian's CDN contracts
- Ongoing channel operations after handover
- Platform certification for any new distribution partner added during the engagement

## 3. Approach and timeline

| Phase | Weeks | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assessment | 1 to 4 | Current state, target architecture, migration sequence |
| Foundation | 5 to 10 | Consolidated scheduling environment, ad-decision integration |
| Migration wave 1 | 11 to 16 | Four channels live on the new stack |
| Migration wave 2 | 17 to 22 | Remaining ten channels |
| Handover | 23 to 26 | Runbook, training, hypercare |

Total: **26 weeks**, commencing 6 April 2026.
