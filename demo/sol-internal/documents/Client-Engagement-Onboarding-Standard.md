# Client Engagement Onboarding Standard

**Sol Consulting** · Internal · Version 2.6
**Effective 1 August 2026** · Owner: People Operations, with Information Security

---

## 1. Why this is strict

Putting the wrong person on a client account, or the right person without the right clearance, is the fastest way to lose an account and in two of our sectors it is a contractual breach rather than an embarrassment.

This standard covers what has to be true before somebody is client-facing, and how long each item genuinely takes. **Lead times are the reason this document exists.** Almost every onboarding problem we have had was somebody discovering a fifteen-day check with four days to go.

## 2. Applies to everybody

Complete before the first client-facing day, whatever the account:

| Item | Lead time | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed engagement-specific NDA or BAA | 1 working day | Legal |
| Named on the engagement in the engagement tracker | Same day | Delivery lead |
| Client code of conduct acknowledgement | Same day | People Ops |
| Sol security refresher, if more than 12 months since the last | 45 minutes | People Ops |

## 3. Account-specific requirements

These vary by client and several have long lead times. The authoritative list per account is the client compliance tracker; this section explains the ones that catch people out.

### Public sector (Commonwealth IT and similar)

- **State background check: 15 working days**, and it requires fingerprints taken **in person at a site in the state**. This is the single longest lead time we deal with and it cannot be expedited.
- Client VPN and GovCloud account: 5 working days, and **the background check must be cleared first**. The two are sequential, not parallel, so the real lead time is twenty working days.
- Accessibility training to WCAG 2.2 for anyone touching a user interface.

### Healthcare (Calder Health and similar)

- HIPAA awareness training, annual, certificate sent to the client's security team.
- Business associate agreement acknowledgement.
- **No protected health information in any AI tool.** This explicitly includes SAGE and the internal assistant. There is no approved exception and no redaction workaround.

### Financial services (Brightwater Bank and similar)

- Screening including credit and criminal record: 10 working days.
- **Client-issued laptop. Sol devices are not permitted on their network**, which means ordering a device from the client ten working days out. People arriving with a Sol laptop have been turned away at reception.

### Media and entertainment (Meridian Media and similar)

- Content embargo NDA. Unreleased programming and scheduling are commercially sensitive and have been the subject of a leak investigation at a peer firm.

## 4. The rule that follows from the lead times

**Twenty working days.** If somebody is joining a public sector account, start on the day the staffing decision is made, not the day the engagement needs them. For everything else, ten working days is usually enough.

Where a start date cannot accommodate the lead time, the delivery lead says so to the client before the person is named, rather than after.

## 5. Rolling off

Within five working days of somebody leaving an account:

- Client system access revoked, confirmed by the delivery lead in writing
- Client-issued devices returned and logged
- Engagement material moved to the account's archive folder and removed from personal drives
- Clearances recorded with their expiry, because they are reusable and re-running a fifteen-day check we already hold is pure waste

That last point is worth saying twice. **Clearances are an asset.** Somebody cleared for Virginia eighteen months ago can be back on that account in five days rather than twenty.

## 6. Who to ask

People Ops for clearances and training. Legal for NDAs and BAAs. Information Security for anything touching client data or AI tooling. The delivery lead owns the outcome regardless of who does the work.
