# Staffing Model and Rate Card

**Sol Consulting** · Internal · Version 4.0
**Effective 1 July 2026** · Supersedes v3.2 (January 2026) · Owner: Marcus Bell

---

## 1. What changed in this version, and why it matters

Version 3.2 priced work on a blended onshore and offshore model, with the Hyderabad build pool carrying up to 40% of build hours.

**That pool was wound down on 30 June 2026.** Sol now delivers onshore, supplemented by named partner firms on specific engagements with client consent.

This is the single most consequential change in this document. **Any fee lifted from a proposal or SOW written before 1 July 2026 is under-priced against how we now deliver**, typically by 12 to 18 per cent on build-heavy work. Re-price from this rate card. Do not scale an old number.

## 3. Standard team shapes

These are the shapes our delivery actually takes. A proposal that departs from one needs a sentence saying why.

**Assessment (4 to 8 weeks)**
0.1 Partner · 1.0 Principal · 1.0 Senior Consultant

**Build and migrate (12 to 26 weeks)**
0.1 Partner · 1.0 Principal · 2.0 Senior Consultant · 2.0 Consultant

**Run and transition (4 to 12 weeks)**
0.05 Partner · 0.5 Principal · 1.0 Manager · 1.0 Consultant

The build shape moved from **1 : 2 : 3** under v3.2 to **1 : 2 : 2** here. Fewer consultants, all onshore, and each carrying more. It costs more per hour and finishes sooner; on the engagements we have run both ways the total is broadly similar, but **the per-hour basis is not, and that is what a fixed fee is built on.**

## 4. Discounting

| Discount | Who approves |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 5% | Engagement partner |
| 5% to 12% | Marcus Bell |
| Above 12% | Marcus Bell and the CFO, with written rationale |

Multi-engagement or multi-year commitments may justify the middle band. A first engagement with a logo we want is not, on its own, a reason to go past 12%.

## 5. Margin expectations

| Fee type | Target margin |
| --- | --- |
| Time and materials | 40% |
| Fixed fee, assessment | 45% |
| Fixed fee, build | 38% |
| Public sector, any type | 35% |

Public sector is lower by design: longer procurement, more compliance overhead, and rate cards we do not set.

An engagement forecast to land more than **5 points** under target is raised at the weekly commercial review, not at the end.

## 6. Utilisation

Target billable utilisation is **72%** for Consultant through Senior Manager, and **45%** for Principal and above, the remainder being pursuit work, SAGE and practice development.

Firm-wide utilisation was **68.9%** across the last two quarters. The gap is almost entirely the lag between someone rolling off and being proposed, not a shortage of work. Anyone within four weeks of rolling off should appear in the Monday staffing call.

## 7. Bench

Time on the bench is normal and is not a performance matter. What is expected:

- Bench time is still recorded on a timesheet, against practice development or SAGE
- Anyone on the bench for more than **three weeks** is discussed by name at the staffing call
- Bench is the first place to look when a pursuit needs a body, before we speak to partner firms
