# Expenses and Travel

**Sol Consulting** · Internal · Version 3.4
**Effective 1 July 2026** · Owner: Finance

---

## 1. The principle

We are a travel-heavy firm and most expenses are recharged to a client. That makes two things matter more than they would elsewhere: **what the client's contract actually permits**, and **whether the claim is inside the fee cap**.

Where this policy is silent, apply the test a partner would apply: could this be explained to the client whose engagement it is charged to?

## 2. Recharge and caps

Expenses are recharged at cost and capped as a percentage of fee. The cap is per engagement and is in the SOW.

| Engagement type | Typical cap |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed fee | 8% of fee |
| Time and materials | 10% of fee |
| Public sector | As stated in the client's own travel policy, which overrides ours |

**Public sector is the one that catches people.** Commonwealth IT reimburse at the state's own rates, which are lower than ours on hotels. The difference is not recoverable from the client and comes out of engagement margin, so book inside the state rate or expect the delivery lead to ask why.

Once an engagement reaches **80% of its expense cap**, Finance notifies the delivery lead. Further travel needs their agreement before it is booked, not after.

## 3. Approval

| Amount, per claim | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to £100 | Line manager approves the claim, no pre-approval |
| £100 to £750 | Line manager, in writing, before the cost is incurred |
| £750 to £3,000 | Engagement partner |
| Above £3,000 | Finance Director |

A cost may not be split across claims to stay under a threshold. Where a single trip exceeds £3,000 in total, the trip needs Finance Director approval even where each element is smaller.

## 4. Deadline

Claims within **45 days** of the date incurred. Beyond 45 days needs Finance Director approval; beyond **90 days** is not paid, because by then the engagement is usually closed and it cannot be recharged.

Expenses are paid with the payroll run following approval. Payroll cut-off is the **18th**.

## 5. Per diem

Applies when away overnight on client business. Receipts are not required within the per diem; anything above it is not reimbursed.

| Location | Per 24 hours |
| --- | --- |
| UK outside London | £50 |
| London | £68 |
| Ireland and EU | €75 |
| United States, outside NY and SF | $90 |
| New York and San Francisco | $110 |
| Rest of world | £75 equivalent |

Where a meal is provided by a hotel, a client or a conference, reduce by **£14 breakfast, £20 lunch, £28 dinner**.

Alcohol is not reimbursable except within an approved client entertainment claim.

## 6. Travel

Rail standard class. First class only where the total fare is lower than the standard fare available at booking, with the comparison attached.

Air: economy under **6 hours** scheduled flying time, premium economy above. Business class needs Finance Director approval and is granted for flights over 9 hours where the person is expected to work the day of arrival.

Mileage: **45p** per mile to 10,000 miles in a tax year, **25p** after. Passenger supplement 5p per mile per colleague.

Taxis where public transport is unavailable, before 06:00, after 22:00, or where you are carrying equipment.

## 7. Accommodation

Book through the Sol travel portal. Caps exclude breakfast and local taxes:

- London £230 · UK other £160
- New York and San Francisco $370 · US other $260
- Other international £190

Where nothing is available inside the cap, book the nearest and note why on the claim. Do not stay somewhere unsafe to stay inside a cap.

## 8. Client entertainment

Needs **engagement partner pre-approval whatever the amount**. Record every attendee by name and organisation. Sol people must not outnumber client attendees.

Gifts to clients over **£50** need Finance Director approval and go in the Gifts and Hospitality register. Never cash or cash equivalents. Several of our public sector clients prohibit gifts of any value, and their rule wins.

## 9. Not reimbursable

Fines and penalties of any kind · personal travel insurance, since corporate cover applies · minibar, in-room entertainment, spa and gym · airport upgrades bought personally · home broadband · anything a client has declined to recharge and no engagement partner has agreed to absorb
